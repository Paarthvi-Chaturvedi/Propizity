Here’s a little more about Propizet.

What is Propizet?+

Propizet is an upcoming proposal creation platform for freelancers, agencies, consultants, and small businesses. It brings writing, design, pricing, and client approvals into one simple workflow.

Can I use Propizet right now?+

This is a preview of the product we’re building. You can preview the template concepts here and join the early-access waitlist through our Google Form. The full application is not available yet, and a launch date has not been announced.

Can I make proposals match my brand?+

That’s the plan. Propizet is being designed with customizable colors, fonts, logos, and layouts, so each proposal feels like a natural extension of your business.

How will AI help with my proposals?+

Planned AI assistance will use your project details to create a structured draft. You’ll stay in control, with the ability to edit, customize, or regenerate individual sections before sharing.

How will I share proposals with clients?+

The planned application will support downloadable PDFs and responsive proposal links, with view tracking, client comments, approvals, and electronic signatures.
